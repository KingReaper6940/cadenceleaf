Supported notation and chord names

Uppercase A–G roots and optional # or b, common qualities, and optional slash bass: [C], [F#m7], [Bbmaj7/D]. [*Softly] and [N.C.] are unchanged annotations. Blank lines stay blank.

Directives: {title: text}, {subtitle: text}, {comment: text}, and matching non-nested {start_of_verse}/{end_of_verse} or {start_of_chorus}/{end_of_chorus}. Lines beginning with # are source remarks. Other directives, grids, diagrams, key/capo/transpose metadata and escape syntax are rejected.

Qualities: major (empty), m, min, maj, dim, aug, sus2, sus4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11, 13, m6, m7, m9, m11, m13, maj7, maj9, maj11, maj13, dim7, m7b5, 7b5, 7#5, 7b9, 7#9, 7sus4, add2, add9, madd9. This is a limited ChordPro-style subset.

Use text you wrote or have permission to process. Cadenceleaf has no song database, scraping, audio or fingering library. It transposes supported bracketed chord names, not tablature or notes.

Spelling follows a simple sharp/flat preference, not harmonic analysis. A zero shift preserves original spellings. Chord-over-lyric rendering can add space to prevent collisions; check line wrapping in your browser or print preview.
